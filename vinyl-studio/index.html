Decal workshop

  1. Load PNG/JPG images from files or a folder. The launcher automatically reads the decals folder. JPG images do not have transparent backgrounds.
  2. Click an image, then a paintable surface on the model. The decal is placed at the actual 3D point on the bodywork.
  3. In Select mode, drag a decal to move it. Use the right panel to resize, stretch, rotate or flip it. Alt+drag or right drag always rotates the view.
  4. Return to any decal in the layer list to rename, hide, reorder, duplicate or delete it. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: undo / redo.
  5. Each car has its own design. Transfer copies your work to another car while preserving the original.
  6. Save an .epvinyl project to keep all images and editable layers for every car. PNG files contain only the merged result. Projects are not saved automatically on close.
  7. Export a 512×512 PNG for one car, or a ZIP for all cars with layers. Empty cars are omitted from the ZIP.

Stock body UV mapping has limitations: shared UV areas may repeat the image on another surface. Glass, wheels and non-paintable parts cannot receive vinyls. Only projects saved by the current editor are supported.
